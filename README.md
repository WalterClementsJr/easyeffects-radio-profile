EasyEffects presets for microphone radio effect, i.e. make your recordings as garbage as you can.
Import and adapt to your preference.

Short summary of what each effect does:

- Minimum sound treatment from `Noise reduction`. Add `Deep Noise Remover`, `Speech processor` if you operate in a more rowdy environment, or rid of it if you are already comfortable with your mic setup. They don't affect audio quality that much otherwise.
- `Bass loudness`: for the muffling effect.
- `Crusher`: add the minor crackle/static when speaking. Modify `Mix`(0-100, the lower the value the clearer the vocal), `Bit reduction`: lower value intensifies the effect.
- `Limiter`: bound the volume, especially from higher frequencies from the harsh sibilance of "s," "z," "sh," and "ch".
Does the job better than `Deesser` in this case since `Equalizer` should be turned to 11.

`Equalizer` is an entire playground on its own.
This is where you'll find most of your time tweaking.
The vocal frequency range optimized for radio is around 300-3500Hz according to my research.
Freely extend it if you want to increase sound quality.

And remember: it is infinitely easier to make your mic sounds worse than to improve it. You, too, can go frenzy on unintelligible jargon while wreaking havoc in someone's ears.
